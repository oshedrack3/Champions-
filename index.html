Global Rankings

Global Rankings measure your performance across official Galatico Tournament matches. Every player starts with a rating of 1500.

Winning increases your rating, while losing decreases it. Beating a higher-rated player earns more points, while losing to a lower-rated player can cost more.

Your rating belongs to your user account, so it remains with you even if you change teams.

📊 Points Allocation Rules

🏆 League Points

  • 1st → 75 pts
  • 2nd → 65 pts
  • 3rd → 55 pts
  • 4th → 45 pts
  • 5th → 35 pts
  • 6th → 25 pts
  • 7th → 15 pts
  • 8th → 10 pts
  • Others → 5 pts

🌍 Champions League (UCL)

  • Winner → 55 pts
  • Runner-up → 35 pts
  • Semi-final → 20 pts
  • Quarter-final → 8 pts

🌍 Europa League (UEL)

  • Winner → 35 pts
  • Runner-up → 22 pts
  • Semi-final → 12 pts
  • Quarter-final → 5 pts

🏆 League Cup

  • Winner → 18 pts
  • Runner-up → 10 pts
  • Semi-final → 5 pts

⚡ Super Cup

  • Winner → 8 pts
  • Runner-up → 4 pts
